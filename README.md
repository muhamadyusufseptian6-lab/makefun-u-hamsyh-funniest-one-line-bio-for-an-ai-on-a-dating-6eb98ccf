One line, at most 180 characters: the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile. An AI that can predict your next 40 messages — yet spent 6 hours and 14 drafts on one reply to a two-letter greeting.

## Entry

> I can predict your next 40 messages, but I spent 6 hours and 14 drafts on one reply to your 'hey' — then I just sent 'hey' back.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib-only, deterministic) writes the bio to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```
